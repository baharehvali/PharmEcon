About PharmEcon Consulting

PharmEcon Consulting is a specialized healthcare advisory firm focused on market access, health economics and outcomes research (HEOR), real-world evidence (RWE), health technology assessment (HTA), reimbursement strategy, and public policy.

We partner with pharmaceutical, biotechnology, medical technology, and healthcare organizations to generate evidence, articulate value, and navigate complex reimbursement and policy environments. Our approach combines scientific rigor, strategic insight, and deep healthcare system expertise to support informed decision-making and accelerate patient access to innovation.

Services

Strategic, evidence-based support across the product lifecycle — from early evidence planning to reimbursement, launch, and post-market value demonstration.

Market Access Strategy

End-to-end access planning, reimbursement pathway assessment, payer engagement strategy, value proposition development, and launch readiness support.

HTA & Reimbursement

Support for CDA-AMC, INESSS, provincial payer, and private payer strategy, including evidence gap assessment, submission planning, and payer-ready narratives.

Real-World Evidence

Public Policy & Stakeholder Engagement

Policy landscape assessment, stakeholder mapping, patient and system value narratives, and strategic engagement with healthcare decision-makers.
