About PharmEcon Consulting

PharmEcon Consulting is a specialized healthcare consulting firm focused on Market Access, Health Economics & Outcomes Research (HEOR), Real-World Evidence (RWE), and Health Technology Assessment (HTA).

Founded by Bahareh Vali, PhD, PharmEcon Consulting brings extensive leadership experience across pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and medical technology sectors. We help organizations generate evidence, articulate value, and navigate complex reimbursement and policy environments to support patient access to innovative healthcare solutions.
